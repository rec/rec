# Tiny research project: electronic calendars

## Goals

* Subscribe to calendar events on Apple or Android phones
* Multiple organizers and calendars
* Cheap
* Simple
* Ethical provider
* European provider

## .ics format

There is only one calendar format today, called .ics, and it's supported by everything
and everyone.

You can provide a URL for Apple users to subscribe to a calendar, but Android users must
use a desktop computer to subscribe to a calendar.

## A provider: Infomaniak

https://www.infomaniak.com/fr/a-propos

* Never heard of them before
* Ticks off all the boxes
* Can probably do this on their free plan for 0 €

## A plan

* One person gets a free Infomaniak account and evaluates it
* Decision point: go/no go
* Calendar event creators also get free Infomaniak accounts and make calendars
* Anyone can subscribe without an account

## Possible downsides

* A public calendar and record of activities for authoritarians to use
* Using free tier means a bad actor can pretend to be in the collective
* All digital things have similar risks
